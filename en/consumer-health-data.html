Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

For residents of Washington, Nevada and other U.S. states with consumer health data laws · Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy is provided under the Washington My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373), Nevada SB 370 and similar laws. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

Categories of consumer health data we collect

None. The health information you enter in Umama (treatment type and dates, medications, doses, shot times and sites, appointments, lab and ultrasound values, notes) is stored and processed only on your own device. It is never transmitted to us or to any server, so we do not collect it.

If you choose to email us and include health information in your message, we receive that information only to answer your request.

Sources

Only you, by entering information in the app on your device or by emailing us.

Purposes

On your device: to provide the app's features you request (reminders, calendar, summaries, reports). In email: to respond to your support request.

Categories of consumer health data we share, and with whom

We do not share or sell consumer health data, and we have no affiliates or third parties with whom we share it. Data leaves your device only when you yourself export or share it (for example a PDF report you send to your doctor).

Your rights and how to exercise them

  • Access and deletion: you can view and delete all of your data in the app at any time (Me → Data → Delete all my data). You can also email support@umama.app to confirm whether we hold any of your consumer health data (for example in a support email), to get a copy, or to have it deleted.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using the app and delete your data, or ask us to delete any support correspondence.
  • We will respond within 45 days, free of charge. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Appeals

If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision or emailing support@umama.app with the subject “Appeal”. We will respond in writing within 45 days. If your appeal is denied, you may contact the Washington State Attorney General at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint or your state's attorney general.
