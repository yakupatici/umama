Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

In short: Umama has no accounts, servers, ads or analytics. The health and treatment information you enter is stored only on your device. As the developer, we do not see, collect, sell or share it.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Umama app for iPhone and Apple Watch (“Umama”, “the app”) and this website. Umama is developed and published by Yakup Atıcı, Türkiye (“we”, “us”). Contact: yakupatc7312@gmail.com.

2. Information you enter in the app

While using the app you may enter:

  • your name (or a nickname) and, optionally, your partner's name;
  • your treatment type, cycle and treatment dates;
  • your medications, doses, shot times, injection sites and whether each shot was taken;
  • clinic appointments, lab values, ultrasound/follicle measurements and notes;
  • medication stock and treatment expenses.

Some of this is health data. It is processed on your device only so the app can work (to schedule reminders, show your calendar and summaries, and build reports). It is not transmitted to us, to a server or to any third party.

3. Where and how your data is stored

  • On your device, encrypted: data is kept in a file protected by iOS Data Protection inside the app's own container. You can also lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode.
  • Widgets and Lock Screen: the summary needed to show your next shot is shared with widgets and the Live Activity on the same device. These can appear on the Lock Screen; the “Hide medication name in notifications” setting (on by default) hides medication names there.
  • Apple Watch: the same shot summary used by widgets is sent directly from your iPhone to your paired Apple Watch (via Apple's WatchConnectivity), and shots you mark on the watch are sent back to your iPhone.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device; no push server is used.
  • Device backups: if iCloud Backup or computer backups are on, app data is included in your device backup together with your other data. These backups are kept by Apple in your Apple Account and we cannot access them. With Advanced Data Protection, your iCloud Backup is end-to-end encrypted.

4. What we do not collect

Umama contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not access your contacts, location, photos or Health (HealthKit) data, and it does not track you across apps or websites. Our App Store privacy label is therefore “Data Not Collected”.

5. Camera and Face ID

The camera opens only when you choose to scan a medication box. The image is processed with Apple's on-device text recognition; it is not saved or sent anywhere. Face ID / Touch ID is handled on the device by Apple; Umama cannot access your biometric data.

6. Subscriptions and Apple

Umama+ is purchased through the App Store. Your payment details are processed by Apple; the app only learns on your device whether your subscription is active. Apple may give developers summary sales reports and, if you have chosen to share them, anonymous usage and crash statistics. This is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

7. When you choose to share

When you create a doctor report (PDF) or a CSV export, the file is created on your device and sent only where you choose (for example Mail, Messages or Files). That sharing is subject to the rules of the app or person you share with.

8. When you email us

If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to reply and resolve your request. Please avoid sending health information unless it is needed. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after the request is resolved and then deleted. Our email provider's servers may be located outside your country; any transfer is made with the safeguards required by applicable law.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Your in-app data stays on your device until you delete it.
  • Use Me → Data → Delete all my data to permanently erase everything. Deleting the app also removes the data from the device.
  • Copies inside earlier iCloud or computer backups remain in your Apple Account until you delete those backups.
  • There is no account, so there is no account to delete.

10. Your rights

Because your data lives on your device, you can view, correct, export and delete it at any time. You can also exercise the rights you have under the law where you live (for example GDPR Articles 15–22 in the EU/EEA and UK, Türkiye's KVKK Article 11, or U.S. state laws: see our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy) by emailing yakupatc7312@gmail.com. We respond free of charge within 30 days (or sooner where the law requires). You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Children

Umama is designed for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

12. Security

Since your data does not leave your device, your device is its most important protection: we recommend using a device passcode and turning on Umama's app lock.

13. Medical disclaimer

Umama is a reminder and logging tool. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnose, or calculate or recommend doses. Always follow your doctor's and clinic's instructions.

14. Changes

When we update this policy we change the date above. We will also announce material changes in the app.
