FAQ

I'm not getting reminders

In Settings → Notifications → Umama, make sure notifications and “Time Sensitive Notifications” are on. Allow Umama in your Focus modes (Sleep, Do Not Disturb). If your time zone changed, open the app once to reschedule reminders. For the trigger shot we recommend an extra alarm.

I don't want medication names on my Lock Screen

Me → Hide medication name in notifications is on by default; notifications, widgets and the Live Activity then show a neutral label instead.

Can I lock the app with Face ID?

Yes: Me → Lock with Face ID.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Umama has no account, so your data moves with your iPhone backup. Set up the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or with Quick Start. You can also keep a copy with Me → Data → Export (CSV).

How do I delete my data?

Me → Data → Delete all my data. This can't be undone. Deleting the app also removes the data from the device.

How do I send a report to my doctor?

Me → Data → Doctor report (PDF) creates a PDF with your protocol and shot history that you can share by email or message.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Umama+, or use this link. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

My purchase is missing / I switched devices

Tap Restore on the Umama+ screen and make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account.

I'd like a refund

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

My clinic's date differs from the app

Retrieval, transfer and test dates in the calendar are estimates. When your clinic gives you a date, add it with Calendar → + → Add appointment and the estimate is replaced automatically.
